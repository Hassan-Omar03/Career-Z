15: 2. Project Vision
16: 
17: CareerZ is a production-grade, AI-powered global education ecosystem designed to connect every stakeholder in education through a single intelligent platform.
18: 
19: Instead of creating another job portal, LMS, school management system, or marketplace, CareerZ aims to become the complete digital infrastructure for education worldwide.
20: 
21: Every student, parent, teacher, institution, employer, education agent, donor, trainer, academy, marketplace seller, and administrator should be able to work together through one unified platform.
22: 
23: CareerZ will be accessible from any country and will never be limited to a single language, currency, or education system.
