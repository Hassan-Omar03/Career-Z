# Student and parent manual

## Create your account

1. Choose **Sign Up**, then Student or Parent.
2. Verify your email, then upload the documents the verification page asks for. You can add several files to a document. If you do not have a required document, choose **I don't have this document**, upload another paper and explain why.
3. Choose **Submit**. A CareerZ admin reviews your account. You are told by notification and email.
4. After approval, complete **My Profile**. The dashboard opens when it is complete.

## For students

### Join an institute

- Search from **Institutes** on the website, open the institute and apply. Track it under **Applications** in the dashboard.
- After the institute accepts you and places you in a class section, its published courses for that section appear in **My Courses** automatically. You do not press Enroll on an institute's course.

### Courses and progress

- **My Courses** lists your courses, lessons and resources.
- A teacher's own free course: open it from **Courses** and press **Enroll free**. A paid course is bought first.
- **Learning Analytics** shows your progress. **Offline Study** keeps saved material and notes on your device and syncs when you reconnect.

### Join a live class

1. Open **My Classes**, then **CareerZ Live Classes**.
2. **Join Class** appears when the teacher starts the class. If the teacher uses Zoom or Meet, it opens in a new tab.
3. Allow the camera and microphone if your browser asks.

Attendance is automatic: **Present** within 15 minutes of the start, then **Late**, and **Absent** if you do not join. The class stays open until the teacher ends it. A fee your institute treats as blocking can stop you joining.

### Assignments and exams

**Assignments & Tests** lists work, due dates and exams. Start an exam when it opens and submit it before it closes. Results appear after the teacher or institute publishes them.

## For parents

### Link a child

Open **My Children** and enter your child's account email. The student approves the link from **Parent Connections**, or the institute links you.

### Follow your child

| Menu | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Attendance | Daily and class attendance |
| Results / Grades | Published results |
| Homework / Assignments | Work and due dates |
| Exam Schedule | Upcoming exams |
| Live Transport | The school bus, when the institute uses tracking |
| Health Record | Medical information and incidents |
| Permissions & Consent | What you allow for your child |

### Pay fees

1. Open **Fee Management** and choose the fee.
2. Choose **Pay by bank transfer**. Pay CareerZ's service fee to the account shown and upload the receipt.
3. When CareerZ confirms it, pay the institute the amount shown and upload that receipt.
4. CareerZ checks and approves. The fee shows as paid and a receipt appears under **My Receipts**.

### Meet a teacher

Open **Parent-Teacher Meeting**, choose the teacher and a time, and send the request. If the teacher accepts, the meeting appears there. An online meeting has a join button.

## If you have a problem

Use **Institution Help Desk** for your institute, or **Help Center** for CareerZ.
