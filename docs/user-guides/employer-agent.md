# Employer and agent manual

## For employers

### Post a job

1. Sign up as an Employer and get approved.
2. Open **Post a Job**. Add the details, an optional image, and the posts table.
3. Submit. The job goes live after an automatic check. Only a job that looks suspicious waits for an admin.

### Applicants

Under **My Jobs**, open a job to move applicants through Screening, Shortlisted, Interview, Final interview, Offer and Joined.

- Interviews default to the CareerZ video room. You can also use Google Meet, Zoom, Teams or your own link.
- A job with the type **Freelance** receives proposals with milestones. See the [Freelancer Hub guide](freelancer-hub.md).

### Other menus

| Menu | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Employees | People you hired |
| Salary Management | Payslips |
| Promotions & Packages | Featured and urgent jobs |

## For education agents

Agents post jobs and scholarships. They do not handle admissions.

### Post for an employer or institute

1. Under **Post a Job**, name the employer you recruit for. That employer must accept, and the commission rate is agreed at that point.
2. **Institute Requests** holds recruitment requests from institutes.
3. Follow candidates through **Applications**, **Shortlisted Candidates**, **Interviews** and **Successful Hires**.

### Commission

Add your payout account before an agreement starts. When a candidate is hired, the employer pays CareerZ's fee and then your commission, and an admin confirms it. Track it under **Commissions**.

### Visa cases

**Visa Cases** tracks a candidate's documents and stages with a verified consultant.
