# Faculty guide

## Get started

1. Sign up as a Teacher, upload your documents and submit them. An admin approves your account.
2. Complete **Teacher Profile**.
3. An institute invites you from **Invite People**, or you accept its offer under **Employment Offers**.

## Create a course and lessons

- Open **My Classes** to create a course. A course you create for an institute needs a class section from the institute.
- A course must be **published** before students can join it or attend its live classes.
- Publishing sends the course to the students in that class section automatically.
- Use **Learning Material Upload** for lessons, files and video. Students are notified when you publish.

## Run a live class

1. Open **Live Classes**, then **Schedule a live class**.
2. Choose the course, title, start and end. Your own courses show as "My own course".
3. Choose the provider: the CareerZ classroom, or an external link such as Zoom or Google Meet.
4. At class time, press **Start Class**. Only the assigned teacher can start it. **Open Classroom** returns you to it.
5. Use the whiteboard, chat, polls and quizzes. Press **End class** when you finish.

Attendance is saved when you end the class. If your institute turned on the working hours rule under **Institute Settings**, classes can only be scheduled inside those hours.

## Mark attendance

**Student Attendance** is for classes you mark by hand. Physical classes are always marked by the teacher.

## Assignments and exams

| Menu | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Homework / Assignments | Create work, then grade submissions |
| Examinations | Create exams, set the opening time and duration |
| Question Bank | Keep questions to reuse |
| Results / Grades | Enter and publish marks |

## Talk to students and parents

- **Student Communication** and **Messages** reach your own students.
- **Parent-Teacher Meeting** handles meeting requests.
- **Class Engagement** covers questions and polls.

## Pay and other work

- **Salary & Finance** shows payslips.
- **AI Teacher** helps write lessons, quizzes and papers. It needs an AI key under AI settings.
- **Freelancer Hub** lets you offer tuition and other services.
