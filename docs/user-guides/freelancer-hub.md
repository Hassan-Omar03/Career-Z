# Freelancer Hub guide

Any verified account can offer services and any verified account can hire. Open **Freelancer Hub** in the dashboard menu, or **Explore → Freelancer Hub** on the website.

## Offer a service

1. Under **Freelancer Profile**, say what you offer and add your skills and portfolio.
2. Under **My Services**, choose **New service**. Add up to three packages: Basic, Standard and Premium, each with a price, delivery days and revisions.
3. Save. It goes live after an automatic check. Only a service that looks suspicious waits for an admin.

## Hire from a service

1. Open the service and compare the packages.
2. Choose **Order**.
3. In the order, choose **Pay this milestone**. Pay CareerZ by bank transfer and upload the receipt. CareerZ's fee is added on top and shown before you pay.
4. When CareerZ confirms the payment, the freelancer starts and the delivery time begins.

## Hire from a freelance job

1. Post a job with the type **Freelance**.
2. Freelancers send proposals with a fee split into milestones. They appear under **Proposals**.
3. Accept one. It becomes an order, and you pay each milestone when you are ready.

## Deliver and get paid

1. When a milestone shows it is held by CareerZ, do the work.
2. Choose **Submit delivery** with a note or files.
3. The client approves, and the milestone amount goes to your wallet. You withdraw from the wallet.

If the client does not answer within 7 days of a delivery, the payment is released automatically.

## Ask for changes or report a problem

- The client can choose **Ask for changes**. A package allows the number of revisions it lists.
- Either side can choose **Report a problem** while CareerZ holds the money. The money stays frozen until a CareerZ admin pays the freelancer or refunds the milestone amount to the client's wallet.

## Reputation

Clients can review an order after approving work. Your service page shows your trust score, rating, completion rate, response time and complaints.

## Messages and receipts

Each order has its own messages, and the client and freelancer can also use **Messages**. Every confirmed payment has a receipt under **My Receipts**.
