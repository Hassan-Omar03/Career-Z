# Institution guide

## Set up your institute

1. Sign up as an Institute, upload the documents and submit them. A CareerZ admin approves the account.
2. Complete **My Institution**: name, address, logo, programs and public page.
3. Under **Institute Settings**, set the timezone, working days, school hours and classroom rules.

The option **Only allow these inside working days and hours** limits parent-teacher meetings and live classes to those hours. Untick **Live classes** to allow classes at any time.

## People

| Menu | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Invite People | Invite teachers, staff and students by email or link |
| Staff Management | Add staff and their permissions |
| Teacher Management | Link teachers and their employment |
| Student Management | Set each student's class section, roll number and status |
| Parent Management | Guardian links and permissions |

## Classes and courses

1. Under **Classes & Timetable**, create class sections and the timetable.
2. Under **Courses & Batches**, create courses and assign each one a section and teacher.
3. Publish the course.

Students in that section get the course automatically, including students added later. Graduated and transferred students do not. A student who dropped a course stays dropped.

## Admissions

**Admission Management** holds applications. Accepting one creates the student's record, places them in the program's section and creates their fee plan.

## Fees

Under **Fee Management**, set up fee plans, generate billing cycles, record payments and follow overdue fees. Choose per plan whether an overdue fee blocks access, warns only, or does nothing. Fee receipts have a QR code anyone can check.

## Academic calendar and exams

Use **Classes & Timetable** for the timetable, **Examination Management** for exams and results, and **Certificates & Degrees** for issuing credentials.

## Payroll

**Payroll** generates payslips from each employment's salary, allowances and deductions.

**Jobs & Hiring** posts your jobs and handles applicants. **Placement Office** connects students to employers.
